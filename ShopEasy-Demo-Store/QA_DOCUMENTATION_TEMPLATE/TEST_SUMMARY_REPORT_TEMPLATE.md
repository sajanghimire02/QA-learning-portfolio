The application shows good potential as a QA learning tool, and the documented bugs provide excellent practice opportunities for beginner testers.

> **📝 QA Learning Tip:** A Test Summary Report is your opportunity to communicate the overall quality of the product. Be honest and objective. Don't inflate pass rates or hide bugs. A good tester reports facts and lets the data speak. The goal is to provide useful information that helps the team make informed decisions about product quality.
